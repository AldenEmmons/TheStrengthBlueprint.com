# Business Identity

> What makes The Strength Blueprint different. Our unique angle, our market position, our edge.

---

## The Gap We Fill

Physical therapy restores function but rarely delivers performance-level loading — and the discharge comes before the client has a structured path back to the weight room. Generic strength coaching builds load without accounting for injury history, fear-avoidance, or validated outcome tracking. We bridge that gap.

**TSB is the structured return-to-strength program that PT doesn't deliver and general coaching doesn't understand.**

---

## Target Demographic

Adults 30–50 navigating chronic or recurring musculoskeletal pain, injury history, or movement limitations who want to build genuine strength — delivered remotely via Trainerize.

---

## Our Position

At the intersection of:
- Clinical Rehabilitation
- Pain Science Education
- Strength Coaching

No one else owns this intersection. That's our edge.

---

## Who We Serve

- Adults with chronic or recurring musculoskeletal pain who are out of acute care
- Clients with injury history who've stalled in general fitness or are afraid to load
- Clients cleared by a provider but not getting structured return-to-strength programming
- Healthy adults seeking individualized strength and hypertrophy programming with assessment guardrails

---

## Who We Do NOT Serve (Online)

- Acute injuries (<6 weeks post-injury) or post-surgical clients without provider clearance
- Clients currently under active PT care without communication and clearance from their PT
- Clients with red flag symptoms — referred immediately
- Clients whose presentation cannot be safely assessed remotely

---

## Scope of Practice

**We DO:**
- Corrective exercise programming
- Strength and hypertrophy programming
- Movement education and load-management coaching
- Outcome and capacity tracking (PROs, capacity benchmarks)
- Behavior, sleep, and stress lifestyle coaching within CPT scope

**We DO NOT:**
- Diagnose conditions or interpret imaging
- Treat acute injuries, post-surgical conditions, or active medical issues
- Prescribe rehabilitation protocols for clients still under PT care without that PT's coordination
- Provide nutrition prescriptions outside CPT scope
- Manipulate joints or provide manual therapy

## Core Message

**"Still limited after trying everything?"**

We are the missing piece — evidence-based programming that addresses the full picture, not just the symptom. Assessment-driven. Criterion-progressed. Individualized.

## Authority Signals

- Evidence evaluated against a 4-tier evidence hierarchy (Tier 1: systematic reviews, meta-analyses, clinical practice guidelines — drives all decisions)
- Validated assessment tools and outcome measures at every touchpoint: NPRS, PSFS, ODI, DASH, LEFS, capacity benchmarks
- Structured reassessment at W4, W8, W12 — not guesswork

---

## The Differentiator

Assessment-driven. Criterion-progressed. Individualized — not template-and-forget.

Every client is assessed on intake and at every reassessment. Phase progression requires meeting specific, measurable outcome criteria. Programs are built around the individual's data — not assigned by default.

Most providers are excellent at one thing. We integrate all three pillars — clinical rehabilitation, pain science, and strength coaching — within a single, structured remote delivery model.
