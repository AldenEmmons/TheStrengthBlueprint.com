The 3-Phase Method

Here's exactly what you're walking into.

Consistency matters more than exercise type. But neither works without the right load at the right time. Our method follows the clinical evidence — graded exposure to feared movements, biopsychosocial assessment across all four domains, and criterion-based progressive overload your body is actually ready for.

01

Restore. Reduce pain & rebuild baseline capacity.

Graded exposure, isometric loading for irritable areas, pain neuroscience education, and sleep/stress hygiene. Lower volume, sub-threshold loading, updated every 1–2 weeks based on tolerance. We build trust in movement while your capacity rebuilds.

Isometrics  ·  Graded Exposure  ·  PNE

02

Bridge. Load with purpose.

Re-introduce loaded compound movement. Bilateral to unilateral. Partial to full ROM. Tempo work, RIR-autoregulated intensity, high-frequency low-volume loading of previously irritable patterns. We build movement-specific capacity at the pace your body can absorb.

Graded Exposure  ·  Tempo  ·  RIR Autoregulation

03

Build. Drive your specific outcomes.

Hypertrophy, strength, body composition, sport readiness — periodized loading, MEV to MAV to MRV progression, stretch-mediated hypertrophy bias. You graduate when validated outcome criteria say you're ready — not when you feel like it.

Sport-Specific  ·  Testing  ·  Retained Capacity
